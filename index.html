About me

I’m Yuval, a software developer with a mechanical engineering degree. I work on algorithms, Python systems and applied AI.

From July to September 2026, I worked full-time on a UGV defense project. I developed shared Python backend and operator interfaces for command handling, telemetry and diagnostics, with logging across four components, thread-timing diagnostics, watchdogs and heartbeat handling.

At BGR Formula Student, I led planning and control from 2023 to late 2025, sharing implementation with the team. We achieved approximately 50 metres of physical autonomous driving using ego-centric navigation without a functioning IMU.

I like turning an unclear problem into a model I can inspect, a first version I can run, and an experiment that tells me what to change.

scenario
What am I tuning?

PID acts on the heading error to a point 0.8 m ahead on the route. Kp reacts now, Ki accumulates error, and Kd damps changes. Try Kp 6, Kd 0 at high speed, then add damping and compare.

A kinematic bicycle model with a 0.85 m wheelbase, steering limited to ±37°, bounded integral and a fixed 1/120 s step. The car starts 0.35 m off the line. Cone contact, tyre slip, sensors, and real vehicle dynamics are not modelled. This is an educational simulation.

Drag or use arrow keys to orbit. Wheel or +/− to zoom.

Visual perception · experimental lab

Depth from almost nothing.

A moving pattern can feel solid. What gives it depth?

Try this: Play with depth cues at 0, then raise them to 100. Does the apparent rotation become easier to read—or change?

Why this experiment?

This is a structure-from-motion sketch, inspired by the kinetic depth effect. With depth cues at zero, there is no depth shading, and the projection is orthographic. Front and back are ambiguous. The slider gradually adds perspective and front-to-back shading.

Structure blends a rotating dot sphere with a fixed, seeded cloud. Pause and use “Turn the form” to explore without animation. It illustrates a visual problem, not a measure of your perception or a model of the brain.

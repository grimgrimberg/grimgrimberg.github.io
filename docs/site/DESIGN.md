# Portfolio Design Context

## Visual Thesis

Calm engineering command center with maker mischief.

The site should feel like a precise workbench: dark tinted surfaces, quiet
technical structure, strong hierarchy, and small moments of personality. The
fun parts should feel earned by usefulness.

## Color Strategy

Restrained dark neutral base with a few named signal colors:

- Ink: `#05070a`
- Panel: `#10151a`
- Bone: `#f6f1e8`
- Brass: `#d8aa4f`
- Cyan: `#58c7bc`
- Green: `#9bd66b`
- Coral: `#ff766d`

Avoid purple gradients and large decorative color blobs. Use grid, rule, and
surface texture instead of orb backgrounds.

## Typography

Use local, dependency-free system fonts with a mechanical tone:

- Display/body: `Bahnschrift`, `Aptos Display`, `Segoe UI Variable`, system sans
- Technical labels: `Cascadia Mono`, `JetBrains Mono`, `Consolas`, monospace

Large headings should be confident and compact. Body text should stay readable
at 65 to 75 characters max where possible.

## Components

- Panels are low-radius surfaces with subtle full borders, not default glass.
- Buttons may be pill-shaped for touch affordance; content cards should stay
  tighter and more mechanical.
- The command center, terminal, Clippy, and goose are signature interactions.
- Fake reviews should be visibly fictional and secondary to engineering proof.

## Motion

Use short, purposeful transitions on transform and opacity. Avoid bounce,
layout-property animation, and excessive background filters.

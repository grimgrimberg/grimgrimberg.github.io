# Portfolio Product Context

## Register

Brand. This is a portfolio, so the interface is part of the proof.

## Audience

Recruiters, hiring managers, and engineering leads looking for robotics,
autonomy, simulation, control, or AI/tooling candidates. They should understand
the engineering fit quickly, then discover the personality layer if they keep
exploring.

## Primary Goal

Make it easy to decide whether Yuval is worth contacting for engineering roles.
The first-screen promise should be calm competence, not a joke reel.

## Personality

Calm engineering command center with maker mischief. Clippy, terminal commands,
fake reviews, roast mode, the goose, and retro games are personality affordances;
they support the portfolio rather than hijacking it.

## Anti-References

- Generic purple AI-gradient portfolio
- Beige LinkedIn personal-brand page
- Chaotic hacker terminal cosplay
- Over-polished agency site that hides the engineering proof

## Privacy Guardrails

Keep the public surface to the real email, LinkedIn, GitHub, CV access, and
copy/mailto fallbacks. The email must stay visible and usable; privacy is about
not exposing extra personal channels. No phone, WhatsApp, or calendar links on
the public page.
